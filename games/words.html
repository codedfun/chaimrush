ChaimWords

How to play

Guess the hidden word. Pick 4, 5 or 6 letters at the top. Every guess has to be a real word. After each guess the tiles change color:

cloud

Teal: right letter in the right spot.

plant

Gold: the letter is in the word, but in a different spot.

Dark: the letter isn't in the word. There's no daily limit: tap Next word to keep playing.
