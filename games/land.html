ChaimLand

Build anything you like out of blocks. Explore the hills, trees and pond, or build in the open field in the middle.

  • Drag to look around, pinch to zoom, and drag with two fingers to move.
  • Pick a block at the bottom, then tap any block to put the new one on that side.
  • Switch to Break to remove blocks. Tap TNT with Break to light it.
  • Fire next to TNT lights it too. Lamps glow at night.
  • Lay Track, then pick Train and tap the track. Trains follow turns and hills by themselves.
  • Swipe the block bar sideways for more blocks: stone, wood, leaves, water and flowers.
