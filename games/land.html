ChaimLand

Walk, fly and build anywhere in a huge world of hills, mountains, lakes and forests.

  • Use the joystick to walk and the up arrow to jump. You hop up single steps by yourself.
  • Drag anywhere to look around. Pinch to move the camera closer or further.
  • Tap Fly (or double-tap jump) to fly. Hold the up arrow to rise and the down arrow to sink.
  • Pick a block at the bottom and tap a block near you to build on it. Tap the Build button to switch to Break.
  • Put a Train on a track and it drives itself. Tap TNT with Break, or put fire next to it, to blow it up.
