Play it like an app: add it to your Home Screen

iPhone or iPad (Safari)

  1. Tap the Share button in Safari's toolbar, at the bottom of the screen (at the top on iPad).
  2. Scroll down the menu and tap Add to Home Screen . If you don't see it, tap Edit Actions at the bottom of the list and add it.
  3. Check the name says Chaim Rush, turn on Open as Web App if you see that switch, then tap Add.
  4. Open Chaim Rush from your Home Screen. It plays full screen, with no Safari bars.

Using Chrome on iPhone? Tap the Share button in the address bar at the top, then Add to Home Screen.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Tap the ⋮ menu at the top right.
  2. Tap Add to Home screen or Install app, then Install.
